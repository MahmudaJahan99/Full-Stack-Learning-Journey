# Horizontal Rule — `<hr>`

`<hr>` represents a thematic break between sections of content.

Example:

```html
<h2>About Me</h2>

<p>I am learning full stack development.</p>

<hr />

<h2>My Projects</h2>

<p>Here are some projects I have built.</p>
```

Conceptually:

```text
About Me
──────────────
I am learning full stack development.


My Projects
──────────────
Here are some projects I have built.
```

The line is only the **visual representation**. The important part is that `<hr>` tells the browser:

> "There is a thematic change or separation here."
